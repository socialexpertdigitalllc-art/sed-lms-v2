How do I get a quote?

Call us or send a message through the contact page with a few details about your project. We will follow up to talk through what you need.

We serve Eastside, Mill Hill, Harbor Point and the surrounding neighbourhoods. Not sure if you are covered? Just ask.

Can I choose a day that suits me?

Tell us when works best for you and we will do our best to fit your schedule.

What should I have ready when I call?

A short description of the job and any photos you have are helpful, but they are not required.

How will I know what the work includes?

We walk through the scope with you before anything starts, so you know exactly what to expect.
